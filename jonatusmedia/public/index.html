GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I download any Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube video?

No. Downloads depend on content ownership, permissions, and the platform's official features. JonatusMedia does not bypass restrictions.

What quality will I get?

For direct video files, the server transfers the original file without transcoding. Quality depends on the source file.

Does this require an account?

No account is needed for direct-file downloads. Platform-specific exports may require signing into the platform itself.

What file types work?

Direct links serving video, audio, or binary media content are supported, up to 500 MB per file.
